About

GT Movies Store is a Django tutorial project for CS 2340. Browse the movie catalog, search by title, and open a movie to see its description, poster, price, and reviews.

Create an account to write and manage your reviews, report inappropriate reviews, and view your order history. Add movie quantities to your cart and place a simulated purchase. This educational store does not collect payments, rent movies, or stream video.
